Focus mode and the adaptive Pomodoro, plus the break screen that deliberately feels different from work.

**Maps to** `ControlPanel`. Built from `Timer`, `Toggle`, `SegmentedControl` and `Button`.

## Desktop
- Two cards side by side (6 + 6 columns). **Focus mode**, active: elapsed of 50:00, "Ends at 10:31 pm · 19 min to go", the blocked sites with "Edit list", and "Stop focus" / "Add 10 min". **Adaptive Pomodoro** ("Long break after 4"): the `Timer` with its why line and controls. Under them, a "Today's sessions" table.
- Idle setup: duration presets (25 m, 50 m, 90 m, Custom), "Block distracting tabs" ("You can always let yourself through for 5 minutes."), and the Pomodoro length scale (10 fatigued · 15 high · 25 normal · 35 low) with the why line.
- **Break**: the whole canvas switches to the rest palette. There's no sidebar, and no numbers apart from the countdown. "Short break · 3 of 4 done. Step away for a few minutes." A breathing disc with three tips (breathe with the circle, look at something 6 metres away for 20 seconds, stand up and roll your shoulders), "Next: 25 min focus. Your load has eased to 48%.", then "I'm back early" and "Skip break". The disc holds still under reduced motion.

## Mobile
Idle setup → Focus phase running (large ring, why line, controls) → Short break as a full-bleed rest screen.

## Rules
- Focus length comes from load at the moment of start: 10 (fatigue), 15 (high), 25 (normal), 35 (low). Say why every time.
- A long break follows every 4th focus session.
- Starting focus mode while a Pomodoro runs links them: the blocks last until the Pomodoro's focus phase ends.
