The full-page overlay shown over a distracting site during focus mode, gentle, with a deliberate 5-minute escape.

## Layout
A solid `--bg` cover over the page (no blur, so it stays cheap), with a centred card: `ShieldCheck`, the eyebrow "youtube.com is paused", the title "You're 31 minutes into a focus session.", the line "19 minutes to go. This will still be here at 10:31 pm.", and a progress bar.

## Actions
- **Back to work** (primary, takes focus on load): closes the tab or goes back.
- **Let me through for 5 min** (ghost): a deliberate choice. Limited to 3 passes per session ("Used 1 of 3 passes this session"), with "End focus mode" as a quiet link.
- Esc does nothing.

## During a pass
A slim bar pinned to the top of the page: "Pass active · 3:42 left, then this tab pauses again", with "Back to work now". The overlay returns when the pass ends.

## Copy
Never shame. No "Caught you" or "Stay disciplined". State the facts and the time left.
