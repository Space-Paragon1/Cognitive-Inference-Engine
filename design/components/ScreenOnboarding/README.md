Four short steps: what CLR does, the privacy promise, connecting sources, and turning on notifications.

**New flow.** Built from `SourceCard`, `Button` and the step indicator.

## Steps
1. **What CLR does**: "Study with your brain, not against it", then three points with icons: Protects deep focus, Times your breaks (Pomodoros from 10 to 35 min, sized to your load), Reorders your tasks.
2. **Privacy promise**: two lists. What CLR reads (tab and window switches as counts, typing rhythm and pauses, compile errors and their frequency, idle time and scrolling, LMS page visits) and what it never reads (keystroke content, page text, grades, messages or passwords, your camera or microphone). Plain words, no legal tone.
3. **Connect sources**: "Connect where you study", one `SourceCard` per source. Continue is always enabled; "1 source connected. Enough to start. Load estimates get steadier with 2 or more." Back, Skip for now, Continue.
4. **Notifications**: three toggles (break prompts, context changes, overload warnings) and "Turn on notifications". On Android this triggers the system permission prompt; the skip option goes on without it.

## Layout
- Desktop: centred 720px column, step dots on top, Back and Continue at the bottom.
- Mobile: full screen per step, "Skip" top-right, primary button pinned to the bottom above the safe area.
